05 / Currently Building

Currently building

A working translation tool, shaped by a very specific viewing problem.

Reading beyond
the language barrier.

Korean videos. Hardcoded captions. No human-made English subtitles. Autocaptions that miss the point. That was the starting problem.

I’m building a Windows screen-OCR translator. PaddleOCR reads Korean captions from a selected area, and a locally running AI language model translates them into English displayed in a fixed subtitle overlay.

The next challenge is removing the need to select a reading area by hand. I want the app to find dialogue captions wherever they appear while I watch, without mistaking signs or product labels for subtitles.

The challenge: not every word is a subtitle.

The screen contains more than dialogue. The challenge is recognizing which text belongs to the conversation and which can be ignored.

Working today

Today, the Windows app reads Korean captions from a selected screen region, translates them through a local AI model and shows English in a fixed subtitle overlay. The operator controls and component-health checks are functional.

What I’m improving

Next is finding dialogue captions automatically across the video. I’d also like to add Japanese support and, later, recognition of spoken dialogue so the app can work with videos that have no captions on screen.
