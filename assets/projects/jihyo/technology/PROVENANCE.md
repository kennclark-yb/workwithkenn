## Usage and licensing review

These are brand assets, not a claim that the trademarks are open-source or CC0. Google product icons are used for accurate, contextual identification of this project's integrations alongside the existing product names and project explanation. Reviewed Google's [product co-branding guidance](https://partnermarketinghub.withgoogle.com/brands/google/use-cases/product-co-branding/) and [Brand Resource Center](https://about.google/brand-resource-center/products-and-services/). Google's brand remains secondary to the portfolio and project identity. Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Apps Script are trademarks of Google LLC.

Telegram's official logos page permits illustrative uses provided the material does not imply official representation. That permission is distinct from the CC0 dedication on the same page for screenshots. Telegram is a trademark of its respective owner.

Google SVGs are already small web assets; the Apps Script PNG is the official 96px transparent raster used at 32px or less. The Telegram SVG retains the official artwork; editor metadata, whitespace and an unused namespace were removed for size. No artwork paths, colours, gradients or proportions were changed. No runtime third-party image requests are made.
